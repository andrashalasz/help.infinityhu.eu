Invoice-based sales

Updated 2026.08.26 · v2026.08.26

A VAT-registered taxable person is obliged to issue an invoice to the buyer for the supply of goods and services performed domestically for consideration.

Select the customer (or add a new one). In the product search, find the product by scanning a barcode, entering the item number, searching by name, or using the Product search button. An item can also be searched by item number, EAN code, serial number, foreign item number and name.

In the Edit item pop-up window, enter the quantity and, if needed, change the price (both net and gross can be entered). For a product with serial number management, the serial number can be chosen from a drop-down list. The comment cell serves for item-level comments; for internal accounting, a cost centre and a job number can be given both in the header and on the item.

The Save button adds the item to the invoice. The items of an open invoice can be edited and deleted.

On one invoice either a gross item discount or a percentage or fixed-amount total discount can be given. First select the discount type, then enter the amount — the Save button updates the item prices.

The payment deadline is automatically the issue date plus the deadline set on the partner record. The comment field serves for a comment covering the whole invoice. The default payment method can be changed and combined with other methods.

  • For cash payment, automatic printing of the cash desk document can be set

  • For card payment, the card transaction identifier has to be entered

  • For voucher payment, the voucher number has to be entered

  • Every payment method must be added with the + sign; it can be corrected with the red bin sign

The Close button generates the invoice and assigns a serial number. Invoices created this way can be viewed in the Invoicing → Sales menu.

ℹ Note

The information bar on the home page notifies you about sales that were left open and not closed. Closed invoices are submitted to the NAV Online system immediately by a background process.

The invoice can be printed as a PDF and sent by e-mail. When the invoice is issued, the warranty certificate recorded for the product is generated automatically.

Advance invoice and final invoice

Click the Issue invoice button and switch on Advance invoice to create an advance invoice. After that, only the ADVANCE item can be selected.

When producing the final invoice, find the advance invoice in Infinity by its number. The final invoice is produced with the Final invoicing button — the advance appears on it with a negative sign.

Returns

Returns can only be processed from the Invoicing → Sales menu. Clicking the Return button displays the invoice items and the quantities sold. After entering the quantities to be returned, select the method of refund and click Next.

Credit note

After an invoice has been issued, a discount can be granted on the sale with a credit note. A credit note can only be issued from the Invoicing → Sales menu. It can be issued for the full total of the invoice or for individual items.

The Credit note button finalises it. The serial number of the correcting invoice also appears on the original invoice.

Reopening

For changes that do not affect the VAT base (for example comment, cost centre, job number, attachment), the invoice can be opened for editing with the Reopen button.

Reversal

If the economic event did not take place, or an error was made that cannot be corrected, the invoice may have to be reversed. Infinity does not delete the invoice but creates a document with the opposite sign. The reversal can be started from the Invoicing → Sales menu.

The original invoice receives the status Reversed and the correcting one the status Reversal. With the reversal both become settled. If the original invoice had already been paid, the settlement has to be deleted separately.

Copying

With the Copy button you can copy the customer invoice with all of its content. The copied invoice is created with open status and can be edited freely. It receives a serial number with the Close button.

Adding a service job sheet to an invoice

Work completed in other modules can also be invoiced in the invoice-based sales menu. The Add service job sheet button displays the job sheets with status to be invoiced that were issued to the customer in the invoice header. Identical items are placed on the invoice in a consolidated form; the invoiced job sheets receive the status invoiced.

Adding a freight task

The system displays the freight tasks with status to be invoiced that were issued to the customer recorded on the invoice. The same rules apply as for the service job sheet.

Delivered but not invoiced

The closed warehouse movements delivered to the customer (so-called “taken on delivery note” items) can be collected and invoiced at agreed intervals.

Adding a CRM event

With the CRM module, the invoice can be linked to a CRM event — the invoice then appears among the attachments of the event.
