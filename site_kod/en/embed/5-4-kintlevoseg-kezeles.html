5.4 Receivables managementFull page

5.4.1Customer and supplier balances

Open customer and supplier balances can be queried in this menu — separately or together. The list shows cumulative balances.

Clicking the eye button opens the partner record: you can see the issued invoices, the payment movements, and the balances at both invoice and line level.

Tip

On the home page the information bar (subject to permissions) automatically lists overdue customer and supplier invoices, so receivables can be tracked without opening this list.

5.4.2Balance statement

Infinity can print balance statements for your partners — either in bulk or one at a time.

Once you have checked the balances on the receivables management screen, you can use the Notify by e-mail function: this sends the balance statement electronically to every partner that has an e-mail address.

To send to a single partner, type the name into the Search field, select the partner, then send it by e-mail or print it. Sent balance statements are stored and can be retrieved from the Balance statement list menu.

5.4.3Payment reminder

You can send payment reminders to partners for overdue receivables. There are two ways to do this:

  • From the information bar: a reminder can be sent straight from the overdue invoice list, without opening the invoice.

  • From the Payment reminder menu: several invoices can be selected at once, and you can decide which ones receive an informal and which ones a formal reminder.

5.4.4Late payment interest request

The system can send a late payment interest request letter, together with the interest charge itself.

5.4.5Notification frequency for late payment

Here you can set how often the system should send payment reminders or formal notices to partners in arrears.
