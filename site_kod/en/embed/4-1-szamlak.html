4.1 InvoicesFull page

Here you find the list of customer and supplier invoices as well as advance payment requests. You can filter by fulfilment, issue date, due date, partner and comment. Clicking the column headers changes the order of the list.

Recording a supplier invoice or advance payment request

Clicking the invoice type lets you record a new supplier invoice or a new supplier advance payment request in the system.

When recording a supplier invoice or advance payment request, the partner data must be filled in. If the partner does not yet exist in the system, you have to create it.

After selecting the partner, the supplier invoice or advance payment request has to be uploaded.

Then you can add the items. If the item is not yet in the product master data, you can record it as well. Click New item to add an item, or New product to add a new product.

After selecting the product, fill in the price data; you can also enter the cost centre, cost category, general ledger account, project number and a comment.

After saving, the product is added to the invoice.

You can then add further items, remove items, save the invoice as open, or close it. A certificate of completion can be attached to the supplier invoice, and the invoice can also be reversed.

4.1.1Copying an invoice

When a supplier invoice is copied, the original invoice is duplicated in full and gets open status — all of its data can be modified and edited. You can add new items and change the existing ones.

Note

Attaching a document is mandatory when copying as well, because of the filing requirement.

Handling settlements

In the invoice list you can filter for supplier and customer invoices, and within those for settled and open items. Filtering by due date is also possible.

When the invoice is opened, the balance field shows the unsettled total.

Depending on the method of settlement, click the Cash desk or the Transfer button and follow the corresponding process.
