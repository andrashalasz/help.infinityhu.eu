15.9.1Customer purchase statements

It lists, per partner, the purchases made and their details (customer, invoice number, product, quantity, unit price, value of the purchase, date). It can be filtered by date, product, category and customer name.

15.9.2RFM segmentation

It groups the customers on the basis of the Recency (most recent purchase), Frequency (purchase frequency) and Monetary (purchase amount) indicators. It can be filtered by period, products, categories and customer segments.

15.9.3Profit and margin calculations

Analysis of the company's profitability and margin per sales period, product and product category. It contains the data of the period, revenue, purchase cost, profit and margin.

15.9.4Financial movements

Detailed tracking of the company's financial transactions (revenues, expenses, transfers and so on) item by item, in chronological order. It can be filtered by time interval, product, category, cost category and cost centre.

15.9.5Annual, monthly and quarterly closings

A summary of the financial closings in annual, monthly, quarterly and custom breakdown. It contains the month, revenue, costs, profit and margin. It can be filtered by period and currency, and retrieved in XLS format.

15.9.6Plan-versus-actual report

Comparison of financial planning data and actual data. It makes planning possible on the basis of start-up costs, cost centres and divisions, and comparison with the actual data (revenue, expenses, cost of goods sold — with absolute and percentage deviation).
