7 Document filingFull page

The basic purpose of the filing module is to store the documents arriving at the company in electronic form, to track them, to have them certified within the company, to return them, or to approve them for accounting.

Filing types can be created to match the business processes: incoming invoices, interest charges, advances, supplier delivery notes, contracts and so on.

The advantages of filing:

  • During the business processes there is no need to send documents on paper between departments and sites

  • The documents do not get lost and the administrative deadlines are not missed

  • They are accessible and viewable anywhere, at any time

  • The status changes and the processing times can be tracked

  • Filing incoming invoices makes it possible to build efficient checking, approval, authorisation and accounting processes

Filing has two large groups:

  • Final documents: they are only uploaded and categorised (for example product images, user manuals)

  • Documents to be processed: those that require further action (for example incoming invoices, contracts)

During filing, a form is created in Infinity and a photograph or scanned copy of the original document is attached to it. Filing can also happen automatically from the modules — documents generated by the system are placed in the document store automatically.
