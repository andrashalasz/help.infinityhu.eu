4.3.1Cash desk transactions

Recording incoming and outgoing cash desk transactions works in the same way as recording bank transactions.

If the partner has open receivables, these are also listed below and can be linked to the cash payment. The revenue can also be recorded on its own, without linking it to an invoice.

As a first step, specify the cash desk, then the date, currency, amount, comment and the paying partner. The Close button finalises the transaction.

Caution

A cash payment is only possible in an open cash desk period.

After closing, you can print the cash desk document, which the system generates automatically. The system is also able to issue a receipt.

In the cash desk menu you can also create a cash desk report (1), create cash desk periods (2) and close the till (3).

A complete cash desk report can be produced from the issued cash receipt and cash payment documents, including the denomination breakdown of the closing cash balance, per cash desk. The cash desk report can cover any period (daily, weekly, monthly).

4.3.2Cash desk periods

This menu shows every cash desk opened by the company, the periods belonging to them and their current balance.

For the sake of auditability it is advisable to create cash desk periods within the cash desks. Only one period can be open per cash desk. Before opening a new one, the previous one must be closed.

Data required to open a cash desk period:

  • Selecting the cash desk: from the list of cash desks

  • Opening date: the system suggests the current date (can be changed)

The Close button closes the period. In a closed period, transactions cannot be recorded or modified. A closed period can be reopened if the open period has been closed beforehand.
