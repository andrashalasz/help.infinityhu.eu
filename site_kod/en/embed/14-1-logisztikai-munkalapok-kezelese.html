14.1 Managing logistics job sheetsFull page

Logistics job sheets support the management of device movements and documentation related to service activities. It is possible to record new job sheets, take in arriving devices, forward them to the service, and handle returns and DOA procedures.

14.1.1Listing and searching job sheets

In the list, filter conditions (date, partner, status) can be given, the results can be sorted and the columns customised. The individual settings can be reset with the Default or Reset functions. The configured views can be saved and deleted.

14.1.2Creating a new logistics job sheet

Data to be entered: the data of the distributor, the customer and the service partner; the basic data of the product (manufacturer, type, warranty status); the fault symptom, accessories and comments; device identifiers (IMEI, serial number and so on). The system generates the job sheet number and the related dates automatically.

14.1.3Actions available on a job sheet

  • Saving, copying, reverting (with a report)

  • Viewing the process history

  • Managing documents

  • Printing (job sheet, barcode)

  • Deletion

14.1.4Creating a goods-in list

The arriving job sheets have to be assigned to a delivery note:

  1. Select the partner and mark the related job sheets

  1. Enter the number of the delivery note

  1. The system assigns the job sheets to the delivery note

⚠ Caution

Without a delivery note, the job sheet cannot be forwarded to the service.

14.1.5Forwarding to the service

Job sheets that have a goods-in list can be assigned to a service partner. Select the target service and the job sheets to be forwarded. The system automatically generates the delivery note and the advance notification (pre-alert document).

14.1.6Handling returns

Devices returned from the service can be sent back to the original submitting partner. The process: selecting the partner and the job sheets, finalising the delivery note.

14.1.7Forwarding devices with DOA/DAP status

The module supports sending devices with DOA (Dead on Arrival) or DAP (Dead After Purchase) status back to the distributor. The system automatically lists the job sheets concerned, which can be placed on a delivery note and forwarded.
