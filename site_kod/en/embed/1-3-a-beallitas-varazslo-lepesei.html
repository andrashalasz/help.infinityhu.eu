1.3 Steps of the setup wizardFull page

The wizard starts automatically at the first sign-in. You can also start it at any time later from the System → Other → Setup wizard menu.

1

Selecting the country

Specify the country in which you use Infinity and where you will issue invoices.

2

Entering company data

Enter your company's basic data (company name, address, tax number, bank account number). Without these, invoicing cannot operate in line with the legislation.

3

Setting up sites

If you operate at several sites, enter them here. Even with a single site, fill in the data of the registered office.

4

Defining warehouses

Assign the warehouses to the sites. Under stock type, specify whether you sell from own or consignment stock. To receive returns, set up a separate warehouse and stock type.

5

Selecting currencies

Select the currency or currencies in which you will invoice.

6

Invoice and receipt blocks

By default the system creates one customer and one supplier invoice block. If you need more invoice or receipt blocks, record them here.

7

Entering VAT rates

Enter the VAT rates applied in your business. Ask your accountant for help with this.

8

Recording bank accounts

Record your bank account numbers. Also enter the IBAN and the SWIFT code if you have them.

9

Connecting NAV Online Invoice

Under Hungarian law, issued invoices must be submitted to the server of the tax authority (NAV). Failing to do so may result in a substantial fine. Create the technical user in the NAV online system: https://onlineszamla.nav.gov.hu/felhasznalok/uj/technikai
✖ Mandatory setting

Configuring the NAV Online Invoice function is a legal obligation. If you skip it, the invoices you issue will not reach the authority's system, which may lead to a fine.

10

Enabling e-mail templates

Enable the e-mail templates you want to use and, if necessary, adjust the default texts. Click the “Back to home page” button to finish the basic configuration.

Next steps

After the basic configuration, continue with users and roles (System → Users), then load the partner master data (Office → Partners).
