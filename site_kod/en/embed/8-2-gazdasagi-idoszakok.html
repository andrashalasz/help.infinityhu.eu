8.2 Accounting periodsFull page

The accounting periods control whether analytical and accounting documents can be modified. Two kinds of period can be created:

  • Accounting period: controls whether accounting documents can be edited and created

  • Analytical period: controls whether source documents can be edited and created

This duality is needed so that an open accounting period can be ensured for the accounting team while no more economic events are generated. During stocktaking, for example, it is possible to prevent all document creation except the creation of stocktaking documents.

Data of the list manager: period, first day of the period, last day of the period, accounting/analytical selectable field, status.

Possible values of the status:

  • New: only master data can be created for the period, documents cannot be recorded

  • Open: documents can be recorded and modified

  • Closed: created by a general ledger closing; only reports can be retrieved and documents displayed

The analytical period controls that:

  • On a customer invoice item: the fulfilment date and the document date can only fall within the open analytical period

  • On a supplier invoice item: the fulfilment deadline, the general ledger date and the VAT date have to fall within the open analytical period

  • On warehouse movements: the document date can only fall within the open analytical period

  • The date of bank and cash desk documents can also only fall within the open analytical period

⚠ Caution

Only one accounting period can be in open status at a time. All the others have to be closed.
