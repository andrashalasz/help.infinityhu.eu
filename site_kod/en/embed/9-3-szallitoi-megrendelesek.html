9.3 Purchase ordersFull page

The purchase order module is available in the Orders → Purchase orders menu.

9.3.1Creating a purchase order from the customer order

This is the most common case — in the event of a shortage, it can be started from the customer order:

  • Open the customer order in view mode.

  • Click the + Create purchase order button (at the bottom).

  • The Products awaiting procurement pop-up window appears (with a red header).

  • Check the quantities and the suppliers.

  • Click the + Create purchase order button (blue, at the top).

9.3.2Recording a confirmation

When the supplier has confirmed the order (quantity and deadline), it has to be recorded as follows:

  • Open the purchase order.

  • At the items, click the pencil icon on the left of the row — the “Edit procurement item” page opens.

  • At the top of the page you can see the name of the product, its item number and the current In stock and Reserved values.

  • Scroll down to the Confirmations section.

  • Enter the Confirmed quantity (primary) value and the Confirmed date.

  • Click the + Add button.

  • Save the item with the Save button.

ℹ Several confirmation lines can be added to one item if the supplier confirms in parts — a separate date and quantity can be given for each line.

✓ After the confirmation has been recorded, the red 0 / 259.00 value at the item turns green: 259.00 / 259.00 ✓

⚠ When the Finalise button is clicked, a confirmation window appears: “Are you sure you want to finalise? After this operation, items can no longer be added or deleted.” The operation cannot be undone.

9.3.3Finalisation and goods receipt

Once every item has been confirmed and the shipment has arrived:

  • Click the Finalise button — confirm in the pop-up window.

  • The status of the purchase order changes to Confirmed.

  • To start the goods receipt, click the Closing method button (bottom left).

9.3.4Closing method — two options

Option When Effect
Closing with approval If Check required = Yes The approver also has to accept it
Closing immediately If Check required = No (default) Creates the warehouse movement immediately, stock increases

✓ After closing, press the Reserve button on the customer order — the items previously marked Awaiting procurement now move to Reserved: Final status.
