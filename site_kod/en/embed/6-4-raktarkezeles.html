6.4.1Goods receipt

In Infinity, goods can be received in two ways: individually, based on a delivery note, or in bulk.

Receiving goods individually, based on a delivery note

Select the supplier and the warehouse location where you want to take the goods in. The number of the referenced document (the number of the delivery note or the incoming invoice) has to be given. After filling in the basic data, record the items to be received.

Note

In this menu, only items that have been created beforehand can be received.

If the delivery note contains the price, enter it, or accept the previous purchase price suggested by the system. If the price is not known, it can also be given after the receipt has been converted into a supplier invoice.

If the receiving user only has recording rights, they can send the document for approval. The document stays in awaiting approval status until a user with the appropriate permission approves it.

A goods receipt created this way but not closed can still be modified (in the Warehouse movements list: In progress status). It can be finalised with the Close button — the goods are then added to the warehouse. For a closed receipt you can print a barcode, view and print the document, reverse it, and create a supplier invoice from it.

6.4.1.1Receiving product variants

Two methods are possible:

  • We enter the main item on the receipt — in this case all variants can be received at once at the same price, with a quantity per variant

  • We start receiving the variants directly — in this case an individual purchase price and quantity can be given per variant

6.4.2Goods issue

Moving goods out of a warehouse location, in the course of which the stock of the product decreases. Typical issues: issue due to a sales document, issue due to a stocktaking shortage, scrapping, consignment sale (goods taken to the supplier).

Goods issues are started from the Warehouse management menu. Select the partner and the warehouse location. Place the goods on the issue with the Product search button. After closing, the delivery note can be printed.

6.4.3Warehouse movements – creating a supplier invoice from a goods receipt

Invoicing goods receipts is available from the Inventory → Warehouse management → Warehouse movements menu. Select the receipts to be converted into an invoice, then produce the supplier invoice with the Invoicing button. The converted but not yet finalised invoice can be found in the Finance → Invoice list menu.

Goods receipts can also be invoiced in bulk, and one supplier invoice can be produced from several delivery notes: select the receipts belonging to the same supplier, then convert them by clicking the Invoicing button. The numbers of all warehouse movements appear in the comment field of the supplier invoice.

6.4.4Transfer between warehouses

To move stock between warehouses, first select the source warehouse, then the target warehouse. From the products with current stock in the source warehouse, select the items to be transferred. The transaction is finalised with the Close button.

A transfer subject to approval is also possible: the goods leave the source warehouse when saved, but the system keeps them as stock in transit until they are received in the target warehouse.

6.4.5Scrapping

Used to write worn-out or damaged goods off stock. The reason for the scrapping has to be given (this also appears on the scrapping report). After selecting the items, close the document — the scrapped goods are written off stock with a Scrap issue movement.

6.4.6Stocktaking

As a first step, specify the warehouse to be counted, or upload the stocktaking file (the sample can be downloaded from the stocktaking screen).

After the warehouse has been selected, Infinity lists the stock it contains. If you are only counting a few items, select them manually; if the whole warehouse, click the Select all marker.

After selecting the items, click the Select button:

Once the items to be counted have been selected, the warehouse movements can be locked. Click the Lock warehouse button and specify the start of the lock.

Caution

During the lock, no sales, goods issue, goods receipt or transfer between warehouses can be performed from that warehouse. If someone attempts it, the system stops with a warning message.

Enter the physically counted quantities on the stocktaking sheet. Items can be searched for in the Product search box. The Difference column shows the deviation between the quantities in Infinity and those actually counted.

If you find an item that is not in the system, you can look it up in the Add product field and add it to the list (only an item recorded beforehand can be added).

Once every item has been counted and recorded, click the Close stocktaking button. The system then lists the items showing a deviation separately. Clicking the Print stocktaking report button gives you the report as a PDF.

To write off a deviation: in the Inventory → Warehouse management → Stocktakings menu you can encounter stocktakings with three statuses:

Started A stocktaking sheet that has been started but not yet closed
Completed A closed stocktaking
Written off A closed stocktaking with the stocktaking deviations written off

With a stocktaking in Completed status you can do two things: accept the results and press the Write off deviation button, or have it recounted with the Reopen button. After reopening, the quantities recorded earlier are already there; only the recounted quantities have to be entered.

6.4.7Stock receipt

In this menu you can receive, check, correct and approve the imported stock.

6.4.8Stock receipt pending items

If products are added to stock with approval, you can approve and finalise them here. After the approval, the products are added to stock and become sellable.

6.4.9Stock movements

The stocks in the system can be moved (for example from consignment stock to own stock). Select the warehouse and the stock, add the items, then specify the target stock and the quantity. After saving, the transaction becomes final.
