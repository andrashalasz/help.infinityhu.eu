Infinity is a modular enterprise resource planning system in which the integration of the individual areas allows every department of the company to work with a single management system.

Since it is web-based software, it can be reached from anywhere — all it takes to use it is an internet connection. It can be operated on a personal computer, a tablet and a mobile phone as well.

The result of its operation is a well-structured, efficiently managed business with up-to-date information, working with the shortest possible reaction time. Its main characteristics per module:

Administration

  • Management of several sites

  • An unlimited number of users

  • Setting up individual user permissions

  • Task management module

  • Full customisability

Partner management

  • Unified master data management

  • Recording custom data defined by the user

  • Retrieving fresh, up-to-date partner data from the company register

  • CRM integrated into every process

  • Online document management, paperless office solutions

  • Recording work schedules, managing leave, printing attendance sheets

  • Creating Google forms

  • Automatic e-mail communication integrated into the business process

CRM

  • Customer rating on the basis of individually customisable criteria

  • An automated sales process that can be built on workflows

  • Establishing and supporting marketing campaigns

  • Task allocation integrated into the business process

  • Recording the working time of employees and assigning it to tasks

  • Efficient customer service

UNAS webshop management

  • Managing an unlimited number of webshops in parallel

  • Uploading products

  • Immediate, automatic refresh of stock changes

  • Electronic invoicing at the press of a button, or automatically

Sales

  • Creating, recording and tracking quotations and orders

  • Creating an order from a quotation, managing reservations

  • Several document blocks; stock-based sales (with a delivery note) and service invoices

  • Individual or template-based recording

  • Handling unique (factory number) and serial number barcodes, use of a barcode reader

  • Integration of web sales, UNAS website integration

  • Recording and accepting customer cards, vouchers and coupons

  • Creating an advance payment request or an advance invoice, final invoicing

  • Fixed or percentage discount, individual pricing possibility for both receipts and invoices

  • Issuing invoices in a foreign language; recording documents in foreign currency, an unlimited number of currencies

  • Handling settlements in foreign currency

  • 6 basic document templates, each of which can be adapted to individual needs

  • Data provision for tax authority audits

  • Sales statistics filtered by period, product group, outlet and salesperson

  • Partner current account card, margin summary of sales

  • Retrieving reports to Excel or PDF

Inventory

  • A customisable product master data with an individual structure, tree-structured product group codes

  • Serial number management (unique or non-unique)

  • Monitoring of minimum stock and minimum margin; uploading an image to an item, printing a barcode

  • Individual or bulk (Excel-based) item creation and modification; product variants

  • FIFO stock management, operating any number of sites and warehouses

  • Managing stock in different ownership, handling web reservations

  • RF data collectors, barcode readers — eliminating manual data entry

  • Stocktaking with a stocktaking sheet, blind stocktaking; preparation of the environmental product fee and Intrastat returns

  • Time-series records of purchase prices; stocking shops and sites, distributing goods

  • Current stock per item in quantity and value; aged stock analysis

  • Retrieving reports to Excel or PDF

Finance

  • Managing forint and foreign currency cash desks, handling change, cash desk periods

  • Assembling payment batches, data connection with the electronic banking terminals

  • Managing customer and supplier current accounts; forint and foreign currency bank accounts; compensations

  • Handling roundings, exchange rate differences and revaluations

  • Periodic cash desk report with denomination breakdown

  • Financial correspondence: payment reminders, compensation letters, late payment interest letters, balance statements

  • Receivables and supplier liabilities in total; VAT report; aged analysis

  • Retrieving reports to Excel or PDF

Accounting

  • The possibility of importing data from almost any system

  • NAV online connection, two-way communication; automatic tax number validation

  • Business processes that can be closed, opened and separated with the help of accounting and analytical periods

  • Automatic postings; handing over posted data to the accounting program

Service

  • An unlimited number of fault types

  • Service activity and spare parts that can be recorded on a job sheet

  • Separation of warranty and paid activity

  • Quotation with acceptance by e-mail; handling a unique identifier; template job sheets

  • Invoicing that can be started from a job sheet

  • Documents related to service activities (job sheet, handover report, warranty letter and so on)

  • Reports: average service interval, service duration per service technician, consolidated service report

Freight forwarding

  • Route planning, recording freight tasks, automatic road toll calculation

  • Automatic preparation and sending of the confirmation and the transport contract

  • Fleet availability; invoicing that can be started from a freight task, bulk invoicing

  • Automatic stock posting from a freight task

  • Reports: invoicing records, truck table, ratio of loaded kilometres

Fleet management

  • Records of vehicle documents and services

  • Two kinds of fuel consumption calculation in line with the NAV standards

  • Route records, electronic trip logs, vehicle tracking

Document management

  • Storing documents, contracts, orders and confirmations related to the operation of the organisation

  • Handling documents directly linked to an invoice

  • Filing documents in digital filing registers

Authorisation

  • Comprehensive management control and tracking

  • Establishing individual authorisation protocols

  • Building a multi-level approval system

  • Authorisations integrated into the business process, even differing per process

  • Managing cost centres and projects; comments attached to forms

HR

  • Managing employees, handling electronic employment contracts and amendments

  • Online contracting, payroll preparation

  • Online records of employment documents

  • Records of shifts, work schedules, divisions, wage supplements and employee benefits

  • Digitalised HR processes

EIS – Executive Information System

  • Business performance data

  • Cash flow reports

  • HR planning

  • Margin report
