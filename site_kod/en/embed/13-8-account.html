13.8 AccountFull page

Accounts manage the digital profiles of the company's business relationships and corporate customers.

To create a new account, click the button:

You can specify whether you are creating a main element or subordinating it to an existing account (hierarchical relationship). Type: personal (private individuals) or business (with company data).

Business account — data typical of a company:

The role of accounts: central management of customer and partner data, sales and order management, contact persons and documents, credit and receivables management, customisable permissions, integrated workflows.
