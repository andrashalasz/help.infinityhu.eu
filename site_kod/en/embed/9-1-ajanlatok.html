9.1 QuotationsFull page

Following an enquiry from the customer, a quotation is prepared and sent to the customer; if the response is positive, the order is recorded. Quotations issued to customers are recorded in the Orders → Create quotation menu.

When preparing a quotation you can specify the freight forwarder partner, the payment method and currency, the estimated delivery time, the discount and the end of validity. Items recorded in the product master data can be included at the recorded prices, at prices reduced by a discount, or at an individual price. The items added to the quotation can be edited and deleted afterwards.

After recording, the quotation has to be closed — it can then be printed and sent out. The items on the quotation do not have to be in stock; a quotation does not reserve stock.

The possible statuses of quotations:

Open Editable, non-final quotation
Closed Saved quotation in closed status
Ordered Quotation converted into an order
Failed Its validity has expired, or the partner rejected it
Deleted Withdrawn or reversed quotation

For a returning customer or a similar sale, clicking the “New quotation based on this” button copies the original quotation, while every piece of data remains editable.

Completed quotations can be printed to PDF and sent to the partner manually or with the automatic e-mail function. The partner can respond with acceptance or rejection using the buttons in the e-mail. On acceptance, the status of the quotation changes to Accepted / Ordered and the system automatically creates the customer order. On rejection, the reason for the rejection can also be given.

✓ Accepting the quotation and creating the order is fully automatic — there is no need to create an order manually.
