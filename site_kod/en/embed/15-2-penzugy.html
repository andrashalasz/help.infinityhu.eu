15.2 FinanceFull page

15.2.1VAT report

A VAT analysis can be queried for any selected period. In the case of foreign currency invoices, the amounts are listed converted to the accounting currency at the exchange rate recorded at the time of the sale.

15.2.2Rounding invoice report

Used to check the rounding write-offs — it collects the roundings per invoice.

15.2.3Aged customer and supplier analysis report

It shows the customer arrears sorted into predefined time intervals. The intervals are: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, 181–360 days and over 360 days. It can be filtered by customer name, fulfilment and creation date, and a reference date can be given against which the intervals are examined.

15.2.4Sales statistics

A report that can be queried per stock type and sales channel, and filtered by product, warehouse and product category.

15.2.5Sales summary report

A report designed specifically for retail providers, broken down by payment instrument and site.

15.2.6Loan report

The list of loans granted and received by the company, for any period.

15.2.7Loan interest report

The interest list of the loans, for any period, filterable by partner and loan agreement. Available in XLS format.

15.2.8Cost category report

You can query the list of cost categories recorded for the invoices and transactions. The cost category and the time interval have to be selected.

15.2.9Cost centre report

You can query the list of cost centres recorded for the invoices and transactions. The cost centre and the time interval have to be selected.

15.2.10Cost category / cost centre check report

You can check whether a cost category or a cost centre has been assigned to every transaction.

15.2.11Exchange loss/gain report

You can query the exchange losses and gains of transactions accounted for in foreign currency, compared to the exchange rate at the time of issue.

15.2.12Balance sheet

A draft balance sheet can be produced by specifying a time interval and a currency. Available in XLS format.
